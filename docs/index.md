# Practice Area Page Template for Law Firms

A section-by-section template for law firm practice area pages: what to put on the page, in what order, and why. Published by [Legal Web Agency](https://legalwebagency.com/).

Practice area pages are where most law firm leads start. Someone searching "truck accident lawyer in Dallas" or "how to get custody of my child" lands on one of them, decides within seconds whether the firm handles their problem, and either calls or leaves. A good practice page answers that person's real questions in plain language and makes the next step obvious. For examples, read our guide to [law firm practice area pages](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-practice-area-pages/).

## One page per case type

Before writing, decide the page list. One page per case type people search for (car accidents, truck accidents, slip and fall) beats one broad "personal injury" page, because each page can answer that searcher exactly. Start with the case types that bring the most valuable cases, and add others over time.

## The template

| # | Section | What goes in it | Length |
|---|---------|-----------------|--------|
| 1 | H1 | Case type + city: "Houston Truck Accident Lawyer" | 1 line |
| 2 | Short answer | Who you help, with what, and the next step | 40 to 60 words |
| 3 | Call to action | Phone number (tappable) and a short form or button | 1 block |
| 4 | "What to do now" | The first steps the reader should take today | 4 to 6 bullets |
| 5 | How the case works | The process, step by step, in plain language | 300 to 500 words |
| 6 | Deadlines and law | The filing deadline and key rules in your state | 150 to 250 words |
| 7 | What it may be worth / cost | Factors that affect value or fees, without promises | 150 to 250 words |
| 8 | Why this firm | Experience in this case type, how you work, real proof | 150 to 250 words |
| 9 | Reviews or results | 2 or 3 relevant ones, with any required disclaimer | 1 block |
| 10 | FAQ | 5 to 8 real questions with short answers | 300 to 500 words |
| 11 | Related pages | Links to attorney bios, related practices, location page | 1 block |
| 12 | Final call to action | Repeat the phone number and form | 1 block |

## Notes on each section

**Short answer (2).** Write it so it could stand alone in a search result or an AI answer. Answer-first pages are easier for Google and AI tools to quote; see [Google AI Overviews for law firms](https://legalwebagency.com/google-ai-overviews-for-law-firms/).

**Call to action (3 and 12).** On mobile, the phone number should be visible without scrolling. See [law firm calls to action](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-website-call-to-action/).

**Deadlines and law (6).** State the deadline accurately for your state and date the page, then review it when the law changes. Out-of-date legal content can mislead readers.

**Value or cost (7).** Explain the factors, not a number. Avoid "maximum compensation" and similar promises; check the rules on our [attorney advertising rules by state](https://legalwebagency.com/attorney-advertising-rules-by-state-law-firm-website/) hub.

**Why this firm (8).** Be specific: the courts you appear in, how quickly you return calls, who the client will actually speak with. Avoid "expert" and "specialist" unless your state allows it. Link to the attorney's bio; see [attorney bio page content](https://legalwebagency.com/attorney-bio-page-content/).

**Results (9).** Results need the disclaimer your state requires. See [the case results page guide](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-case-results-page/).

**FAQ (10).** Take the questions from intake calls, not from a keyword tool, and add FAQ structured data. See [law firm website FAQs](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-website-faqs/) and [schema markup for law firms](https://legalwebagency.com/schema-markup-for-law-firms/).
